Nicolas is a 5th-year PhD candidate and Graduate Research Assistant in the Frantz Research Group at Washington University in St. Louis. Originally from San Antonio, TX, he earned his B.S. in Chemistry from the University of Texas at San Antonio. His doctoral work focuses on organic chemistry and catalysis, recently leading to a co-authored publication in *ACS Catalysis* detailing a novel collaborative project with Bristol Myers Squibb. Outside the lab, Nicolas enjoys hanging out with his family, brewing beer, and making music.
