Isa is a 4th-year PhD student and Graduate Research Assistant in chemistry at Washington University in St. Louis. A native of Kyle, TX, she holds a B.S. in Biochemistry from the University of Texas at San Antonio, where she was also the conference-championship-winning team captain of the Division I women's soccer team. Her current research focuses on the synthesis of small molecules to develop new, innovative treatments for women's cancers. In her free time, Isa loves playing soccer, cooking, and spending time with her family and friends.
